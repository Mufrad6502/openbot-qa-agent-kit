# Project knowledge

## Scope and environment

Record authorized URLs, build, roles and test-data boundaries.

## Verified product map

For each finding, record the source, observation date, confidence and evidence.
Distinguish observed behavior from requirements and assumptions.

## Journeys and business rules

Record prerequisites, steps, outcomes and dependencies.

## API clues

Record approved endpoints, contract references and sanitized observations.

## Open questions and coverage gaps

Track what has not been verified. Discovery findings are not confirmed defects.

## Run history

Reference dated reports and evidence rather than overwriting past results.
