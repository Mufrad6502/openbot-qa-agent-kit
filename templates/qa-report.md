## Findings

For each finding: ID, title, severity, requirement/source, prerequisites,
reproduction steps, expected and actual results, reproducibility and evidence.
Separate suspected defects, confirmed defects and observations.

## Coverage gaps

Document blocked, untested and excluded flows. Never turn unknowns into passes.

## Evidence index

say so. Export to PDF and inspect the rendered pages before attaching in OpenBot.
